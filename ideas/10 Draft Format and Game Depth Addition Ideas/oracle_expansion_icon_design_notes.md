# Oracle TCG Expansion Species Icons — Design Notes

Summary of icon design session for the 30 new species across Expansion 1 (Shadow Realms) and Expansion 2 (Celestial Ascent). Actual SVG attempts excluded — see Inkscape files for final tracing.

---

## Physical Constraints

- **Icon size:** 12×12mm max (fits on card)
- **Stroke width:** ~0.2pt for main outlines, ~0.15pt for interior detail lines
- **Reference model:** existing dragon icon (base game) — ~6-8 major stroke segments, bold silhouette, minimal internal detail, strategic use of negative space

## Key Lesson from This Session

Initial concepts (first pass) were far too detailed/illustrative for the actual print size — they read fine as descriptions but would be illegible at 12mm. Revised concepts should cap around **8-10 stroke segments per icon**, with a **single strong focal point** rather than multiple competing details.

**Second lesson (from the Revenant iteration):** simple geometric shapes (rounded skull, circular eyes) tend to read as "cute" (Tamagotchi/Pac-Man ghost) rather than menacing, even with correct anatomy. What actually signals "revenant" (undead *warrior*, not generic ghost/skeleton) is the addition of a recognizable **armor/warrior element** — a helmet band, pauldrons, chainmail hints — combined with **angular** (not rounded) hollow eye sockets. A hand-drawn reference sketch supplied mid-session confirmed: skull + armor pieces reads correctly; skull alone does not, regardless of angularity. Symbol design for menacing/undead species should default to including a piece of gear or armor as the identifying feature, not rely on facial geometry alone.

General takeaway for the remaining 28 icons: expect 2-3 iterations per icon is normal, especially for species where "creepy/menacing" is the target feel — rounded shapes trend cute by default and need a deliberate offsetting element (armor, angularity, asymmetry) to avoid it.

## Design Principles for the Full Icon Set

1. Maximum 8-10 distinct stroke segments per icon
2. No stroke thinner than 0.15pt
3. Let negative space do the work — avoid filling every area with line detail
4. One clear focal point per icon: identify the single thing that makes this species recognizable at a glance
5. Test at actual size (12mm) before considering a design final — don't judge only from an enlarged preview
6. Avoid parallel lines closer than ~0.5mm apart (will blur/merge at print size)
7. Favor bold curves over small details — a single well-placed curve reads better than several tiny marks
8. For menacing/undead/monstrous species specifically: add a concrete gear/armor/weapon element rather than relying on rounded vs. angular facial geometry alone

## Per-Species Concept Notes (simplified, 12mm-scale versions)

**Order F — Shadow Light**
- *Bloodmage:* blood-drop outline with 2-3 small spiral/swirl lines inside suggesting flowing magic
- *Revenant:* skull with angular hollow eye sockets, nasal cavity, **plus a warrior element** (helmet band, pauldrons/shoulder armor) — see lesson above. Still unresolved as of end of session; user is hand-tracing in Inkscape.
- *Spectre:* flowing ghost silhouette (rounded top, wavy bottom hem), two dot eyes, minimal detail

**Order G — Deep Light**
- *Deep Gnome:* pointed hat triangle + round head, simple beard curve, small crossed pickaxe shape
- *Stone Troll:* hunched heavy silhouette, small eyes, simple tusks (V-shapes)
- *Forgeborn:* blocky humanoid outline with 3-4 geometric "seam" lines, angular head

**Order H — Crystal Light**
- *Dryad:* simple female profile with 3-4 branch lines as hair, a couple of leaf ovals
- *Crystalkin:* diamond/gem body shape with angular facet lines, triangular limbs
- *Gemsoul:* central hexagon/diamond with 4-6 small floating crystal shards around it

**Order I — Tide Light**
- *Merfolk:* upper-body silhouette + single smooth S-curve tail, 2-3 fin lines
- *Deepborn:* head with 3 simple gill lines on neck, webbed hand outline
- *Coralsmith:* simple figure with 3-4 Y-shaped coral-branch details on shoulders/head

**Order J — Forge Light**
- *Golem:* blocky rectangular body/limbs, single rune circle on chest, featureless head
- *Fleshcraft:* humanoid outline with 3-4 stitch cross-hatch marks, asymmetrical feature
- *Ironbound:* half-face/half-mechanical split down the middle, simple gear symbol

**Order K — Storm Light**
- *Phoenix:* bird head profile + single large upward flame-shaped tail feather, 2-3 flame lines
- *Thunderbird:* spread wings (simple M-shape), zigzag lightning bolt on body
- *Sylph:* flowing female silhouette, two simple curved wings, one ribbon-of-wind line

**Order L — Radiant Light**
- *Angel:* simple head, two feathered wings (outer curve + 2-3 internal lines each), small halo circle
- *Archon:* helmeted head with simple crest, upright sword (cross shape), minimal wing outlines
- *Seraph:* central circular body, 6 simple radiating wing curves, single eye/flame at center

**Order M — Wild Light**
- *Griffon:* eagle head/beak, lion body outline, one visible wing with 2-3 feather lines
- *Manticore:* lion head with mane curves, raised barbed scorpion tail
- *Chimera:* lion head as focal point, small secondary goat head (horns only), snake-tail S-curve

**Order N — Star Light**
- *Celestial:* humanoid silhouette with 4-6 small 4-pointed star shapes on/around body
- *Astral:* floating ghost-like form, 2-3 wavy transparency lines, a few star dots inside
- *Starseer:* triangular robed figure, staff with star tip, small third-eye mark on forehead

**Order O — Life Light**
- *Treant:* organic tree-trunk outline, simple face (dots + curved mouth), 2-3 branch arms with leaf shapes
- *Satyr:* human upper body, bent goat legs with visible joint, curved horns, simple pan-pipes shape at mouth
- *Dríade:* female face emerging from tree, 2-3 bark-texture curved lines, 3-4 simple flower shapes in hair
